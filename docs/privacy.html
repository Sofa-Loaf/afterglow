Afterglow privacy (v0)

Afterglow is free. There is no Stripe, no IAP, and no account. Sold by 28to3.me. Android package me.to28.afterglow.

What we collect — feature only, not sold

This v0 scaffold stores data on the device. It does not upload residue and does not sell data.

DataWhenWhySold
Precise locationAfter you allow foreground GPSLeave trigger + ~15 m / 50 ft listen gateNo
MicrophoneOnly after you start an 8–12s voice noteLeave a voice afterglowNo
Photos / cameraOnly when you take or pick a stillLeave a still. A face is not requiredNo

What we do not do

No account. No feed, likes, comments, or stars. No live “who’s here.” No background location. No ads or analytics SDK. No payments.

Retention

Afterglows expire (default 14 days) and are purged locally. Uninstalling the app removes the on-device store.
